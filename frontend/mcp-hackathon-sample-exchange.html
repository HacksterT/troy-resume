2The timing flag: worth resolving first

You said OIG is visiting next month, but the public record doesn't match that. The most recent inspection was a Healthcare Facility Inspection (HFI) on Dec 9 to 11, 2025 (report 25-04252-256, published Aug 28, 2026), and the routine ~3-year cycle puts the next window at roughly late 2028: an estimate only, since OIG publishes no schedule.

So "next month" is almost certainly one of these, not the routine cycle:

One error

A date. Report 21-03311-15 was published in November 2022, not November 2021. The "21" in a report number is not its publication year.

Worth knowing

"Closed" on the day of publication. Both recommendations show a closure date equal to the report's publication date. That usually means the facility fixed the issue before the report came out. The date records OIG's closure, not when the work finished.

Worth knowing

VISN 9 is the former numbering. VA replaced its 18 VISNs with 5 on July 15, 2026, and OIG tags the August 2026 report under the new VISN 3. "VISN 9's broader record" still works for history before the change, but a VISN filter mixes the two maps, so a regional comparison is safer by state.
