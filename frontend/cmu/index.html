AI Pathways

A shared journey for the Health Administration Division's faculty and staff: learning to use AI well in our own work, and teaching well in classrooms where students already use it.

What this is

AI Pathways has two paths. Learn builds the skills of a confident, careful AI user: how these tools work, how to brief them, how to check what they give back, and how to work well on the platform you use. Teach applies AI to course preparation and to the classroom.

Each path has six stages of two weeks each, so a path runs about one semester. Every stage has the same four parts: a short video or reading, a 15-minute practice task on your own work, a short post about what you found, and an office hours session.

You do not need to do every stage or keep to the calendar. Start where you are. If you are new to AI, start with Learn.

Where things are

The division's AI Pathways team in Microsoft Teams holds the stage materials, the video, prompt, and reading libraries, the self-check that helps you choose where to start, and the discussion. This site holds the office hours session guides: what we review, where people tend to get stuck, and the questions we discuss. Each guide stays here afterward as a quick review.
